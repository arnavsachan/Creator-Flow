# CreatorBuddy AI — Master Engineering Roadmap

This document outlines the full 12-phase production delivery plan for **CreatorBuddy AI**, an enterprise-grade AI copilot and analytics platform for digital content creators.

Status Legend:
- `NOT STARTED` — Planned phase awaiting prerequisite completion
- `IN PROGRESS` — Actively being implemented and validated
- `BLOCKED` — Stalled by dependencies or external factors
- `PASS` — Fully implemented, tested, and verified

---

## 12-Phase Master Plan

| Phase | Title | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | **Project Foundation & Frontend Scaffolding** | Clean mono-repo setup, Vite + React + TypeScript initialization, Tailwind CSS, core types, state containers, and reusable UI/state primitives. | `PASS` |
| **Phase 2** | **Senior Product Design Overhaul & Architecture** | Strict neutral carbon system (`#09090B`), Linear/Vercel/YouTube Studio aesthetic, precision sparkline KPI cards, Recharts velocity chart, evidence triage, video table, and 10 secondary workspace pages. | `PASS` |
| **Phase 3** | **Backend Architecture & Core API Setup** | High-performance backend foundation (FastAPI/Python or Node), modular router design, environment configuration, database connection, and health probes. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 4** | **Authentication & Channel Connection** | Secure OAuth 2.0 integration for YouTube & social creator accounts, token lifecycle management, and multi-channel profile switching. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 5** | **Data Ingestion & Analytics Pipeline** | Real-time and batched metric collection (views, watch time, CTR, subscriber velocity, retention curves), with data state flags (`DEMO`/`LIVE`/`STALE`). | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 6** | **AI Content Ideation & Title Generator** | LLM-driven viral topic discovery, title generation with CTR scoring, angle differentiators, and thumbnail visual prompts. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 7** | **AI Scriptwriting & Hook Optimization** | Deep script generation engine, 5-second hook retention analysis, structure pacing, call-to-action suggestions, and teleprompter mode. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 8** | **Competitor & Niche Trend Intelligence** | Semantic clustering of trending competitor videos, keyword gap discovery, outlier performance detection, and opportunity alerts. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 9** | **Community Sentiment & Comment Mining** | NLP comment analysis, audience sentiment distribution, frequently asked question extraction, and automated high-value reply drafts. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 10** | **Sponsorship & Revenue Valuation Engine** | Creator media kit generator, dynamic CPM/RPM valuation calculator, brand outreach pitch templates, and deal ROI estimator. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 11** | **End-to-End Testing & Reliability** | Rigorous unit, integration, and E2E test suites; handling network dropouts, stale data transitions, and graceful degradation. | `NOT STARTED` |
| **Phase 12** | **Production CI/CD & Cloud Deployment** | Containerized builds, production optimization, CDN caching, security auditing, and live telemetry monitoring. | `NOT STARTED` |
